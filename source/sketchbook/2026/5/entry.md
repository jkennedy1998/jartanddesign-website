## description
Made in 2025, this pixel waveform demo was part of a workflow experiment around using displacement and waveform-driven motion to speed up audio visualization. I was making a lot of music at the time, so the goal was to build animation systems that could react quickly and help me produce music videos faster without needing to hand-animate every beat from scratch.
