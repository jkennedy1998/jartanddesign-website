chord writer

press the exact notes you'd actually play on guitar or piano, hit save chord, then stack the cards into verses, choruses, or whatever sections you're working out.

computer keyboard instrumentation

guitar on your laptop keyboard. the bottom row is the low E string, z is fret 0, each row above is the next string up. hold shift for an octave up, control for an octave down. sound starts on your first press.

octave : 0 hold shift = up · hold ctrl = down
